Rewrite metrics, by hour

Summary

381 commits landed on v-rust in 119 hours, inside a 2,528-hour span. 54 pull requests were merged. Of those hours, 37 were the Rust implementation, 12 were acceptance tests, 10 were the notebook, and 40 were the web UI. Usage in hours that have a commit was 819 million tokens and $12.36 on demand.

The day bin is the same commits and the same usage export, rolled up by date.

5 Jul 2026 18:28 UTC through 19 Oct 2026 02:57 UTC. Hours worked are UTC hours with a commit on v-rust. Hours since start is that whole span. Commits are origin/v-next..origin/v-rust. Tokens count an event only when its UTC hour has a commit on some ref in this repo.

$12.36 is five on-demand events in 14 Oct 15:00 UTC, model grok-4.7. Included events have no dollar amount in the export. Of the 819 million tokens, 782 million are cache reads and 4.4 million are output.
