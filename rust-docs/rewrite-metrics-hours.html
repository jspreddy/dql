Summary

381 commits landed on v-rust in 119 hours, inside a 2,528-hour span. 54 pull requests were merged. Of those hours, 23 were the Rust implementation, 14 were Rust improvements, 12 were acceptance tests, 10 were the notebook, and 40 were the web UI.
